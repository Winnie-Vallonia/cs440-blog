## Key Idea

ECMP spreads traffic across multiple paths in a data center, but its random selection makes it difficult to quickly move traffic away from a problematic path. 
The paper introduces P-ECMP, which gives operators more precise control over the paths selected traffic takes while keeping ordinary ECMP for the rest of the traffic. 

## Critique

One strength that stands out is that the authors tested P-ECMP through simulations and deployed its failure recovery use case in a real data center serving live traffic. 
However, they acknowledge that limited data prevented them from testing more outage events in one comparison. 
Testing a wider range of failures would make their claim about better failure recovery more convincing.

## Connections
The background paper introduces a data center network design with multiple paths between servers. 
P-ECMP builds on this setting by addressing how traffic uses those paths, especially when some traffic needs to avoid a problem. 
Together, the papers show why having multiple paths is useful and why controlling their use also matters.

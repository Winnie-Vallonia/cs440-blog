## Key Idea
Determining whether failures will degrade a network’s performance requires comparing its normal operation with its behavior under failure. The paper explains that this is hard because the number of possible failure scenarios and traffic patterns grows rapidly as the network becomes more complex.

The main contribution is Raha, a tool that analyzes failures and traffic shifts to help operators identify potential performance problems before outages occur.

## Critique

One strength of Raha that stands out is that it was tested on a real production network in Africa and on additional larger network layouts. The results show that considering a broader range of failure combinations helps it identify serious performance problems that tools checking only a few failed links might miss.

However, one limitation is that the larger networks they tested use failure probabilities from the authors’ own network, which may not reflect failure patterns in other networks.

## Connections

The connection with the B4 background paper is that B4 demonstrates how traffic engineering can use network capacity efficiently by distributing traffic across multiple paths. Raha complements this by analyzing how failures and traffic shifts can degrade such networks, connecting efficient operation with reliability.
